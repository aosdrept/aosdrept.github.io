FOR THE AOSD TEAM

Prepare, review, publish

Members prepare events and projects in the editor. An administrator reviews the proposed version and approves publication. The website stays unchanged until approval.

Open the Pages CMS editor

For editors

  1. Open “Evenimente și proiecte”. Choose “Add an entry” or open an existing activity. Invited members sign in by email and do not need a GitHub account.
  2. Prepare the content. For a new activity, save a draft with a Romanian title and “Include în versiunea propusă” switched off. Complete Română and English, dates, links and photographs as you work. Enter times in Romanian local time.
  3. Propose the activity for publication. Complete both languages, enable “Include în versiunea propusă” (Include in the proposed version), then click “Save”. “Evidențiază pe prima pagină” proposes the homepage announcement; if several are selected, the most recent announcement date wins.
  4. Follow the review. Saving runs automatic checks and prepares a version for the administrator. Correct any errors and save again. A new save replaces a pending review.

Changes to an already published activity also require approval. Switching off “Include în versiunea propusă” removes it from the website in the next approved release; it does not retain that activity’s previous version on the website.

Checks and pending releases

For the administrator

  1. Open the latest “Publish website” run. Sign into GitHub with the designated reviewer account. Initially, this is aosdrept.
  2. Read “Publication review”. The summary shows changes since the last live version, Romanian and English text for changed activities, and date warnings. Follow the comparison link to review all changes, including images and withdrawals.
  3. Review the whole batch. Approval covers the entire website version, not one activity. Confirm the facts, translations, organiser approval, image rights and absence of private data.
  4. Approve or reject. Choose “Review deployments”, select “github-pages”, then “Approve and deploy” or “Reject”. If rejecting, tell the editor what needs correction. A subsequent save requires a new review.
  5. Check the published result. Once publication finishes, check both language versions. Failed checks or a rejected release leave the previous website online.

What happens automatically

Each save checks required information for included activities, date formats and ordering, photograph descriptions, internal links and safe formatting. It prepares the review report and flags past deadlines. Approval is tied to the reviewed version; a superseded version cannot be published.

After approval, the website generates both languages, the homepage announcement, and the calendar and archive, sorted by date and grouped by year. Only photographs used by included activities enter the website.

What people review

Editors and the administrator confirm factual accuracy, translation and image-description quality, registration destinations and conditions, photograph permissions and the activity’s actual status. Technical checks do not establish these facts.

After an activity takes place, mark it “Încheiat” (Completed). For cancellations, choose “Anulat” and explain in both languages. Passing the date does not change status or close an external form. The announcement date does not schedule publication. Warnings are generated during checks; there are no daily messages or automatic social media posts.

Drafts are not confidential

Saved files and photographs are public on GitHub before approval, including when “Include în versiunea propusă” is switched off. Do not save signatures, private data, membership applications or internal records. The draft setting controls inclusion in the website; approval controls publication of the version.

Access and help

The administrator invites editors by email through “Collaborators” in Pages CMS. Only reviewers designated in GitHub’s approval rules can approve publication. Editors do not receive the association’s account password.
