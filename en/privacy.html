NOTICE FOR WEBSITE VISITORS

Website privacy

How personal data is handled when you visit the AOSD website or send us an enquiry.

Updated 13 November 2026

Who is responsible for your data

Asociația Ovidiană a Studenților la Drept (AOSD, the Ovidian Association of Law Students), based in Constanța, is the controller of the data you send it through correspondence. For questions or to exercise your rights, email aosdrept@gmail.com.

This notice reflects the website’s current setup and covers visits and general email enquiries. Applications and membership are covered by the separate privacy notice for applicants and members (PDF, Romanian), which sets out the relevant data, legal bases and retention periods.

When you visit the website

You can read pages and download documents without an account or completing a form. In its current setup, the website has no contact or document-upload forms, audience analytics, advertising or embedded social media content. Fonts and images are hosted with the website.

GitHub Pages provides hosting. When you visit, GitHub receives the technical data needed to communicate with your browser. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. See GitHub Pages’ data collection information.

AOSD does not maintain its own visit log or receive a list of visitors or their IP addresses through this website. Processing carried out by GitHub for its own purposes, including service security, is explained in the GitHub Privacy Statement, including legal bases, retention and contacts for exercising your rights.

Cookies and browser storage

In its current setup, the AOSD website does not set cookies or save preferences or identifiers in browser local storage. Selecting RO/EN opens the page’s address in that language. Your browser may cache files to load pages faster.

These statements concern the AOSD website; external services reached through links have their own practices. Browsing does not constitute consent to other uses of your data.

If you email us

An email link opens your email application; it does not automatically send a message to AOSD. If you choose to send one, we receive your email address, display name, message, any attachments and correspondence metadata, such as the date and time.

We use the data needed to answer questions and proposals and follow up on the discussion, based on our legitimate interest in communicating with people interested in the association’s activities — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — while respecting their rights and reasonable expectations. Protecting correspondence and defending legal claims may rely on the same basis where necessary. Handling data rights requests or meeting a specific legal obligation relies on Article 6(1)(c) GDPR.

Sending an enquiry is voluntary. Without a reply address and the information needed to understand it, we may be unable to resolve it. For general enquiries, do not send national identification numbers, identity documents, passwords, health data or academic records. We do not use general correspondence for profiling or solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Who may access data and where it is processed

Correspondence is accessible to authorised AOSD personnel as needed for their responsibilities and to the email service provider, Google (Gmail). Where required by law or for legal claims, strictly necessary data may be disclosed to authorities, courts or the professionals involved. Received messages are not published in the website’s GitHub repository.

GitHub and Google services may involve processing outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. The providers explain applicable mechanisms, such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses, and how to request details of safeguards in GitHub’s transfer information, Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s data transfer frameworks. You can ask AOSD for clarification about the services used.

How long data is kept

For general correspondence, retention depends on the time needed to resolve the enquiry and follow up on the discussion. We consider the nature of the enquiry, any legal retention obligations and a specific need to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim. Data that is no longer needed is deleted or anonymised; correspondence is not kept indefinitely simply because it was received.

Membership applications follow the periods in the applicant and member notice. Providers describe retention for their own technical processing in their policies; AOSD does not determine the retention period of GitHub Pages logs.

Facebook, Instagram and other websites

Links to Facebook, Instagram and other external resources take you to separate services. This website does not load Facebook or Instagram widgets. When you follow a link, the destination service may receive connection and browser data, and its privacy policy applies. For requests about data sent to AOSD, use the email address above.

Your rights

Subject to the GDPR’s conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure or restriction of processing. Data portability applies where its legal conditions are met.

Right to object: you may object to processing based on legitimate interests on grounds relating to your particular situation. Email your request to aosdrept@gmail.com. We respond without undue delay and within one month; if an extension of up to two further months is necessary, we will explain the reasons within the first month.

You may complain to the Romanian National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP) and seek a judicial remedy. For GitHub’s or Google’s own processing, you can also use the contacts in their policies.

Updates to this notice

This page describes how the website currently works. We will update it before introducing features or new purposes involving personal data. The date at the top identifies the latest update.
