Civil law moot court

A practical civil law exercise organised by the Faculty of Law and Administrative Sciences at Ovidius University, with AOSD’s participation, as part of Legal Professions Week.

Who can take part

Third- and fourth-year students, in teams of two.

After registration closes, teams will receive the case they need to solve by the stated deadline.

Dates and deadlines

Team registration
14 November 2026, 23:59
Case solution deadline
21 November 2026, 23:59
Event date
8 December 2026

The registration form specifies 8 December. The activity is part of Legal Professions Week (7–11 December 2026). The announcement reviewed does not specify a time or venue.
