01 / WHO WE ARE

Law brings us together.
Our values define us.

AOSD is an autonomous, academically focused and merit-based student association for law students at Ovidius University of Constanța.

We build our community through responsibility, cooperation and respect for thoughtful work. We encourage initiative and active participation in the association.

AOSD does not act as an institutional representative of students or of the University.

Who can apply?

  • Students enrolled in a bachelor’s, master’s or doctoral programme in Law at Ovidius University of Constanța.
  • An applicable average grade of at least 8.50 and no more than two failed or outstanding subjects.
  • Acceptance of the Statute and the Rules of Organisation and Operation (ROF), and compliance with the rules on incompatible roles.

The annual average from the last completed academic year is assessed. For first-year students in a study cycle without an annual average, the admission grade is used. Eligibility is checked at submission and at the final decision, under AOSD’s governing documents.

YOUR PATH TO MEMBERSHIP

  1. 01

    Read the documents

    Read the Statute, the ROF and the personal data processing notice.

  2. 02

    Submit your application

    Email your completed and signed application, together with the required supporting documents, to AOSD.

  3. 03

    Join the trial period

    Once approved for the trial period, you take part in 21 calendar days of participation and assessment.

  4. 04

    Receive the decision

    You become a member only upon the final decision accepting your application.

The trial period may be extended once by up to 30 days under the ROF. The applicant is informed of the extension and its reason.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A little more clarity.
One step closer.

Answers to help you begin your journey with AOSD.

What should I read before applying?

The AOSD Statute, the Rules of Organisation and Operation, the membership guide and the personal data processing notice. Request the current versions and the application form at aosdrept@gmail.com.

What does the trial period involve?

Assessment covers engagement, quality of work, responsibility, cooperation, initiative, compliance with the rules and conduct. During this period, you have no voting rights and cannot hold positions reserved for members.

How do I send my application data?

Applications are sent by email as described in the application documents. This website does not collect applications through a form. No ID copy, personal identification number (CNP), criminal record certificate, photograph, passwords or authentication codes are requested. Read the AOSD data notice before sending personal data.

Can I withdraw my application?

Yes. You may withdraw your application in writing at any time before becoming a member. Withdrawal does not prevent a future application.
