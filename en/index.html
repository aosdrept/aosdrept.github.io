01 / WHO WE ARE

Law brings us together.
Our values define us.

AOSD is an autonomous, academically focused and merit-based student association for law students at Ovidius University of Constanța.

At AOSD, involvement starts with members’ ideas and contributions: research, writing, communication and organising approved projects. We value well-founded arguments, initiative and following through on commitments.

AOSD does not act as an institutional representative of students or of the University.

02 / OUR GUIDING VALUES

Veritas. Integritas.
Excellentia.

I.

Truth

We research, check sources and support our ideas with evidence. Academic dialogue makes room for different perspectives and criticism expressed in good faith.

II.

Integrity

We respect our colleagues, protect confidential information and disclose conflicts of interest. We take responsibility for mistakes and help put them right.

III.

Excellence

We care about accuracy, quality and deadlines. Academic merit goes hand in hand with dedication, cooperation and a willingness to learn.

LIFE IN THE ASSOCIATION

Ideas worth
taking further.

As a member, you can propose projects and contribute to approved activities according to your interests, experience and availability.

Research and writing

You can contribute to research on legal topics, the preparation of materials and academic initiatives, with care for sources and accuracy.

Projects and organisation

You can develop an idea into a proposal with clear objectives, activities and resources, then help organise approved projects as part of a team.

A step towards
your community.

Download the documents

Who can apply?

  • Students enrolled in a bachelor’s, master’s or doctoral programme in Law at Ovidius University of Constanța.
  • An applicable average grade of at least 8.50 and no more than two failed or outstanding subjects.
  • Acceptance of the Statute and the Rules of Organisation and Operation (ROF), and compliance with the rules on incompatible roles.

The annual average from the last completed academic year is assessed. For first-year students in a study cycle without an annual average, the admission grade is used. Eligibility is checked at submission and at the final decision, under AOSD’s governing documents.

YOUR PATH TO MEMBERSHIP

  1. 01

    Read the documents

    Read the Statute, the ROF and the personal data processing notice.

  2. 02

    Submit your application

    Email your signed application and supporting documents. The Admissions Committee checks eligibility and reviews the application; it may request further documents or a clarification discussion.

  3. 03

    Join the trial period

    Once approved for the trial period, you take part in 21 calendar days of participation and assessment.

  4. 04

    Receive the decision

    You become a member only upon the final decision accepting your application.

The trial period starts on the date communicated to the applicant. If objective circumstances prevent a meaningful assessment, it may be extended once by up to 30 calendar days, with notice to the applicant and an explanation of the reason.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Membership,
made clearer.

From your first question to becoming a member.

Can I apply in my first year?

Yes, if you meet the statutory criteria. If you do not yet have an annual average in your current study cycle, your admission grade for that programme is used. Prepare proof of that grade and the documents required to verify the other criteria.

Does meeting the criteria guarantee admission?

No. Eligibility is checked before the application is assessed and does not automatically grant access to the trial period. You become a member only upon the final admission decision, following the assessment required by the Statute and ROF.

What are my rights and responsibilities as a member?

You can participate in activities, propose projects, request information, vote and stand for election under the Statute. You are responsible for commitments you accept, respectful conduct, confidentiality and protecting data and resources. Academic eligibility must be maintained and evidenced annually under the Statute; relevant changes must be reported.

What does the trial period involve?

You undertake clear tasks reasonably suited to your experience. Assessment covers engagement, quality of work, responsibility, cooperation, initiative, compliance with the rules and conduct. You are entitled to explanations, respectful treatment and an impartial assessment. During the trial period, you have no voting rights and cannot hold positions reserved for members.

Are there any mandatory fees or contributions?

No. The admissions process is free. Becoming a member, remaining a member and exercising membership rights are not conditional on fees, dues or mandatory financial contributions.

What is an incompatible role?

The membership guide identifies teaching, seminar-leading or examining roles at UOC in relation to eligible students. Occasional academic participation, mentoring or research are not, in themselves, incompatible without the teaching or decision-making authority covered by the Statute. Ask for clarification about your circumstances before applying.

What data should I leave out?

No ID copy, personal identification number (CNP), criminal record certificate, photograph, passwords or authentication codes are requested. Read the personal data notice and send only the documents needed for your application. This website does not collect application files through a form; applications are sent by email.

Can I withdraw my application?

Yes. You may withdraw in writing at any time before becoming a member. Withdrawal is not a rejection and does not prevent a future application.
